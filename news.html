EarthPayment Expands EPT / SOL Liquidity

EarthPayment has expanded the liquidity of the EPT / SOL trading pool on Raydium to support improved market liquidity and future trading activity.

An additional liquidity contribution has been made to the EPT / SOL pool, consisting of:

  • 388,763.161 EPT
  • 2.672 SOL

Liquidity Expansion

The additional liquidity strengthens the available EPT / SOL market and provides additional liquidity for users trading EPT on Raydium.

This liquidity expansion does not change the maximum supply of EPT. The maximum supply remains:

100,000,000 EPT

Purpose of the Liquidity Addition

  • Strengthening the EPT / SOL liquidity pool
  • Improving available market liquidity
  • Supporting future EPT trading activity
  • Continuing the development of the EarthPayment ecosystem
